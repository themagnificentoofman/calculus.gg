# Practices retained from the final D/L asset review

These entries describe observed local review successes. The final v4 source
joins, root peer reviews, and strict render are complete; they do not claim
production activation or completion of the global release evaluator.

| Observed success | Rule to keep | Evidence and gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reading the literal radical choices exposed a fixed second-largest answer that the simple numeric parser could not see. | A parser's non-applicability never clears a mathematical relation between choices. Follow up a flagged menu with literal comparisons and preserve the reason a repair became necessary. | The old recurrence menus satisfy `m−1 < sqrt(m(m−1)) < m < m(m−1)` (or use a negative distractor). `recurrence-options-v1` binds all ten old IDs and exact pair differences. Root's `recurrence-option-peer-v2.json` and the final destination-ID screen pass. |
| The ten rational-sequence menus lost their correct-only expanded-power length cue when all choices used the same unexpanded powered-rational form. | Use comparable mathematical forms for every option, and derive each wrong value from a concrete mistake. Test the complete repaired family for new clues. | `rational-sequence-options-v1`: 60 exact differences, 14 negative guards, same-form lengths, strict render; root peer `rational-sequence-option-peer-v1.json` independently verifies all 30 wrong routes. |
| Three option repairs inherited clear current solutions instead of copying historical “Here m” prefaces. | Resolve a new revision against the exact current source-bound presentation before changing its content hash. Preserve the old bytes and presentation binding separately. | `recurrence-options-v1/reviewed-presentations.json` and its source manifest bind all three old content hashes and the prior presentation file hash. The other seven solutions are inherited verbatim. |
| A separate bound found an easier route after the recurrence menu repair: a unit-width interval isolates the only integer choice. | Reassess difficulty with the repaired choices visible. A correct repair can still require a demotion; preserving the old label is not a success criterion. | `recurrence-classification-v1`: complete monotone-convergence/unit-interval proof, 40 exact literal bound comparisons, ten Hard→Medium amendments. Root's v2 peer independently confirms the route and final v4 applies the demotions. |
| Exact joins preserved the same constructions, normalized tasks, and exposure identities across revised IDs. | Revisions and constructor aliases inherit the established normalized task key. Compare old and new capacities under the same policy, including withdrawn records. | `final_prepare.py` fails closed on unreviewed menu changes, lost source joins, changed task counts, missing 47 retirements, or changed D30/244 and L30/241 qualification totals. `source-and-lineage-joins.json` binds the successful merged-v4 execution. |
| Frozen finite APIs reconstructed every candidate and rejected mutated records and invalid indices. | Freeze reviewed source before export, provide an exact finite catalog, and keep amendments in a new source-bound layer rather than changing a previously certified hash. | Both repair bundles expose `catalog`, `build`, and `verify`; each has ten reconstructions and fourteen negative guards. The classification layer is separately hashed and leaves the option snapshot untouched. |

Future authoring must carry these observations into the standing guide and
applicable admission checks. A negative configured screen, a small sample,
or a one-item construction remains limited evidence. Inclusion in this reviewed
inventory does not decide eligibility under the default-off Experimental legacy
preference; root owns that policy and its user-facing label.
