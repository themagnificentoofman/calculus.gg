## Rules supported by this pass

1. **Review the served form and its exact raw source together.** Raw `log` or `operatorname{atan}` strings alone do not describe a menu after the existing notation presenter has run. Record both forms and their source hashes.
2. **Carry the current reviewed explanation into an option revision.** A new hash must not accidentally lose an old source-bound presentation correction. Verify all affected current statements and solution steps, not just the answer menu.
3. **Distinguish text patterns from mathematical claims.** A leading minus in an additive expression is not the sign of its value, and stripping it is not necessarily a full additive inverse. Exact pair proofs and literal surface diagnostics answer different questions; retain both.
4. **Make consistent expression-order repairs across the full family.** The same term-order convention applies to correct and wrong choices. Do not pad or unsimplify just the answer to tune a predictor.
5. **Keep the conclusion consistent with the displayed correct choice.** A value-preserving menu rewrite still needs a matching final line. Preserve the preceding derivation and prove equality of the revised conclusion.
6. **Preserve failures and scoped residuals.** A diagnostic below an automatic applicable-count threshold can still deserve editorial review. Preserve the split, exact inputs, failed assumption, and subsequent independently checkable resolution. Do not relax thresholds or launch new classifier searches to obtain a pass.
7. **Retain useful historical content on evidence.** Exact correctness, clear explanation, defensible options and useful practice at the easiest reasonable level justify active retention. Age supplies neither automatic eligibility nor a reason to withdraw.
