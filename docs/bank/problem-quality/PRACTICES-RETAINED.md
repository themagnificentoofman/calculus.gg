### Final horizon evidence and scarce-family capacity

- Observed success: all 28 pools pass both the development and reserved held-out practice horizons, with continuous attempted history and independently reviewed equivalence. See [complete session evidence](final-session-v1/RESULTS.md). These are offline tests, not player calibration.
- Retained rule: a total unseen count alone cannot establish future round feasibility. When the complete catalog fits the existing bounded probe budget, preserve feasible future family-capped capacity while keeping randomized selection. Never solve depletion by shortening history, renaming duplicates, changing levels, or relaxing broad-pool caps.
- Evidence: the preserved first-development Hard multivariable failures left enough total unseen questions but insufficient per-family capped capacity. The corrected selector passes the same development seeds and all reserved seeds without changing the content. Hard multivariable's 13% headroom remains disclosed.
- Repeatable checks: `tests/selection-quality.test.mjs` includes 25 uneven-inventory sequences; `evaluate-horizons.mjs` retains the full matched contract; `evaluate-scenarios.mjs` adds actual-catalog returning/drill/retirement and retention-bound checks.

### Bounded release metadata

- Observed result: a detailed finite disposition manifest can exceed one SQL statement even when every question payload is bounded.
- Retained rule: store a compact, immutable release manifest that cryptographically binds the complete audit artifact and policy arrays; export and verify the actual policy rows independently. Do not remove reasons, rewrite old manifests, or weaken the admission gate to fit a statement.
- Repeatable check: `tests/quality-delta.test.mjs` verifies that a large policy manifest remains fully bound and its serving representation is bounded. Production import success remains separately required.
