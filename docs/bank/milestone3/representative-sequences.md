# Representative consecutive rounds

The [two playable sequences](representative-sequences.json) are actual ordered output from the ten-round study, not a curated ordering. The full evaluation also records other Olympiad cells. Option ordering is saved per round.

Round 1 begins with a rearrangement problem: the decision is how to couple an increasing weight with a prescribed value distribution. Its Fourier/Mellin integral then calls for a transform identity with convergence control. The binary product instead telescopes into a geometric sum. Later, the Abel equation recovers an integral by reversing a triangular integration order; the variational problem needs a sharp energy bound; the Gamma average uses a functional equation. These are different mathematical decisions, not new coefficients in three kernels.

Round 2 starts with repeated integration by parts and endpoint vanishing, then reciprocal substitution in a quartic denominator. The principal-value item distinguishes ordinary divergence from a symmetric limiting prescription. The boundary-layer limit requires rescaling with domination. The regularization question asks whether an ordinary oscillatory integral converges before evaluating its damping limit. The quadrature defect uses orthogonality rather than evaluating a long polynomial directly.

These samples also show limits: families may recur twice within a round, and broad primary methods may cluster. Their choices are stochastic; the report measures primary methods separately from detailed method labels and alternative solution tags.
