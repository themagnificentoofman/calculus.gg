# Ten source-bound recurrence menu repairs

This separate immutable candidate layer replaces the ten square-root recurrence
menus in the exact v3 D/L preview (eight original-family records and two q2
aliases). All ten retain their statements, answers, Hard classification,
construction, exposure lineage, and current reviewed solutions. The three
retained originals inherit their source-bound reviewed presentation rather than
their historical ambiguous `m` text. The other seven solutions are copied
verbatim, including any existing repeated conclusion. Eight historical
identities remain bound through `previous`; two new private tasks have none.

The prior menus always put the correct answer second-largest. Their radicals
also escaped the diagnostic's simple rational numeric parser. The new choices
are canonical integers, with concrete wrong routes: absolute negative root,
second division by two, omitted quadratic denominator, discriminant square root
alone, radicand constant, or mistaking the initial value for the limit. There
are no arbitrary offsets. The initial-value choice occurs only in three menus;
it deliberately tests the convergence misconception and is readily eliminated
by computing the first iterate. It does not count as a new idea or extra
difficulty.

Correct numeric ranks now have counts 2, 2, 3, 3 across ranks 1 through 4.
All 60 exact integer pair differences are nonzero, and no opposite pairs exist.
The source-ID/construction diagnostic has no flags. IDs and splits were not
changed to suppress a flag. This bounded check does not prove resistance to
every possible option-only classifier; parent independent mathematics and
final destination-ID screening remain required.

`repairs.catalog()`, `repairs.build(family, index)`, and `repairs.verify(record)`
provide the finite API. Original-family indices are 0–7; q2 indices are 8–9.
Do not assume these indices match earlier constructor indices. The source
manifest binds every exact old ID, revision, canonical hash, former review ID,
published predecessor, and reviewed-presentation source. Unknown families,
noninteger/out-of-range indices, and modified records are rejected.

Executed author checks: ten reconstructions, 60 exact nonzero differences,
14 negative guards, and 180 strict source/formatter math fragments with zero
render or semantic-format failures. The previous convergence proof is carried
